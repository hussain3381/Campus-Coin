## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an approachable, empowering, and organized financial lifestyle tailored for student everyday life. The visual narrative combines fresh modern minimalism with energetic, clean utility—dispelling the anxiety and clutter traditionally linked to personal finance.

The aesthetic anchors on crisp porcelain surfaces, high-contrast typography, and refreshing emerald tones that signal growth, fiscal wellness, and vitality. Micro-interactions should feel snappy, lightweight, and tactile. By pairing soft pastel categorization cues with sharp, accessible structures, the interface reduces cognitive load and turns budgeting, splitting bills, and meal-plan tracking into effortless daily routines.

## Colors

The palette balances clean, high-luminance canvases with purposeful chromatic feedback:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base App Canvas: `#f8fafc` (Slate 50)
  - Elevated Cards & Sheets: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
  - Subtle Borders & Dividers: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200)
  - Muted Insets & Input Wells: `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100)

- **Brand & Action (Emerald Core):**
  - Primary Base: `#059669` (Deep Emerald - optimized for accessible button fills and high-contrast badges)
  - Primary Accent / Glow: `#10b981` (Vibrant Mint Emerald - for charts, progress fills, and active states)
  - Primary Hover: `#047857`
  - Primary Soft Container: `#ecfdf5` (Mint Tint)

- **Typography & Neutrals (Slate Range):**
  - Primary Text: `#0f172a` (Slate 900)
  - Secondary Text: `#475569` (Slate 600)
  - Tertiary / Supporting Text: `#64748b` (Slate 500)
  - De-emphasized / Placeholders: `#94a3b8` (Slate 400)

- **Category & Status Badges (Pastel Accents):**
  - Housing / Tuition: `#6366f1` (Indigo) / Container: `#eef2ff`

  - Alert / Negative Balance: `#ef4444` (Rose) / Container: `#fef2f2`

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is specified across all levels for its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and high legibility at micro sizes. 

- **Numerical Display:** Monetary balances and transaction values utilize tight letter tracking (`-0.03em`) and medium-to-bold weights to convey precision without feeling intimidating.
- **Micro Labels:** Category pills and status tags deploy uppercase or title-case `label-sm` with expanded tracking (`0.04em`) to maintain sharp readability on compact mobile screens.
- **Hierarchy:** High visual contrast is preserved by pairing strong bold titles directly with slate-toned body copy, avoiding pure `#000000` to prevent harsh visual vibration against pure white cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive fluid system aligned to an 8pt baseline rhythm:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile (`< 640px`): Single-column feed, bottom sheet drawers, sticky bottom navigation bar, margin of `1rem`.
  - Tablet (`640px - 1024px`): 6-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and margins, persistent icon navigation rail.
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): 12-column grid maxed at `1200px` container width, sidebar navigation, multi-card analytical views.

- **Rhythm Rules:**
  - Card internal padding defaults strictly to `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` (24px) on desktop.
  - Stacked lists and feed items maintain `space-sm` (12px) separation to present clear grouping without disconnecting content.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through clean, tactile layers rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#f8fafc` flat background.
  - **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#ffffff` with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) complemented by an ambient, tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
  - **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, slightly elevating spending cards upon selection or hover.
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Action Sheets):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a semi-transparent slate scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

A friendly, rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`) defines the physical envelope of UI elements:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for structured, confident touch targets.
- **Containers & Cards:** `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) for budget cards, charts, and summary containers, creating distinct soft corners.
- **Modals & Drawers:** `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) along top edges for mobile action sheets.
- **Pills & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for category filters, tags, and small transactional counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#059669` fill with `#ffffff` text, font weight 600. Active click scales subtly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** `#ecfdf5` background with `#059669` text, zero border.
- **Outline:** `#ffffff` background with `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `#0f172a` text, shifting to `#f8fafc` on hover.

### Chips & Category Badges
- Compact pill height (28px - 32px).
- Pair pastel tinted background containers (e.g., `#ecfdf5`, `#fffbeb`, `#eef2ff`) with medium-saturation text and icons.
- Interactive filter chips feature a `1px` border that transitions to emerald when toggled.

### Cards & Ledger Lists
- **Budget Balance Cards:** White cards with `#e2e8f0` border, featuring a top indicator bar in vibrant emerald (`#10b981`) or a dynamic category hue.
- **Transaction Item Rows:** Horizontal layout with a 40px rounded avatar/icon container (pastel background corresponding to category), merchant name in `headline-sm`, relative time in `body-sm` (`#64748b`), and price right-aligned with bold emphasis (`#0f172a` for debits, `#059669` for credits/refunds).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Inset `#ffffff` with a clean `1px solid #cbd5e1` outline, transitioning to an active ring `2px solid #10b981` with an inner glow. Placeholder text in `#94a3b8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `rounded-md` checkbox and round radio, filled with `#059669` and displaying a crisp white checkmark/dot when checked.

### Budget Progress Trackers
- Rounded track (`height: 8px`, `#e2e8f0`) with an energetic gradient progress indicator (`#10b981` to `#059669`). When over-budget, transitions automatically to `#ef4444`.
